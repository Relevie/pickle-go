01

Serve diagonally.

Start behind the baseline and serve underhand, cross-court into the opposite service area.

02

Let it bounce twice.

The serve must bounce once on the return side, then once on the serving side before anyone volleys.

03

Respect the kitchen.

You can play a ball that has bounced in the non-volley zone, but do not volley while touching the zone.

04

Win by two.

Recreational games often go to 11 points, with a two-point lead needed to win. Confirm the format with your group.

A COUPLE OF FRIENDLY REMINDERS

Make the next shot an easy one.

  • Get back to a ready position after every shot.
  • Aim for space and give yourself a comfortable margin.
  • Call the ball early with your partner.
  • Keep it kind; everyone is learning something.

QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know before you go.

Pickleball is easy to start. Here are a few things you might wonder about.

What do I need to bring?+

Comfortable court shoes and water are a good start. If you have a paddle, bring it; many beginner sessions can lend you one.

What is the kitchen?+

The kitchen is the non-volley zone on both sides of the net. You can step into it to play a ball that has bounced, but you cannot volley while standing in the zone.

Can I come if I’ve never played?+

Yes. Look for an all-levels open play or a beginner clinic on the Events page. You can contact us and we’ll help find a comfortable first session.

How does scoring work?+

Most recreational games play to 11, and you need to win by 2. In doubles, the serving team calls the score before serving. Ask your group which scoring format they use.
